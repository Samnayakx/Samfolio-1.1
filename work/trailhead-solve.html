    Work/Trailhead Solve

    Case study ( 01 ) · Trailhead

    An AI-powered re-engagement strategy Trailhead power users preferred over ChatGPT.

    Improving power-user retention with an agentic AI RAG bot: Trailhead Solve, a dashboard redesign and a RAG assistant.

    Role
    Product Designer (Apprentice)
    Timeline
    Aug 2025 – Apr 2026
    Skills
    Conversational UX, RAG workflows, power-user research, dashboard design
    Team
    Team of five with the Salesforce Trailhead product team

    01Overview / Background

    Built for the climb, and it loses experts at the top.

    Trailhead is Salesforce's free learning platform, taking millions of professionals from beginner to certified expert. The Trailhead product team brought us one question: why are the platform's most experienced users showing up less?

    What we found changed the brief: power users weren't unhappy with Trailhead. They were taking their hardest questions to ChatGPT. We ran it like a product bet: kill ideas fast, ship working software, test with real experts. This was a team of five; the research, prototype and media are shared work.

    • Problem: Experts took their questions elsewhere.

      Salesforce's most experienced users were taking their questions to ChatGPT and Slack instead of Trailhead's own community, and between milestones, nothing gave them a reason to come back.

    • Solution: Answers, plus prompts.

      Plain-language answers to real Salesforce problems inside Trailhead, paired with proactive prompts that surface what's relevant next without being asked.

    • Outcome: 6/8

      Given the same Salesforce question, 6 of 8 power users preferred Solve over ChatGPT.

    02Defining the Problem

    The certification path ended, and ChatGPT caught experts on the way out.

    Experts weren't churning in anger. They were quietly drifting, and nothing on the platform noticed. Engagement fell exactly where certification ended, and experts took their hardest questions to ChatGPT. The question we carried into every decision: how might Trailhead become the first place an expert goes when they're stuck, not only when they're studying?

    03Understanding your Users

    We reached 650+ power users and asked where they go instead.

    A first round on opinion gave warm, useless answers, so we switched to behavior: where people drop off and where they go instead.

    • Alex, the Architect · Confident Amplifier

      Opens ChatGPT first when a real problem hits. Certifications are tied to billing rates and performance reviews.

    • Jenny, the Developer · Focused Professional

      Certifications are tied to reviews. Pauses under workload and finds no clear way back in.

    04Your Role and Responsibilities

    Product designer on a team of five, working inside Trailhead's product team.

    The research, the prototype and the media were shared work. We designed in Figma against Trailhead's design system, then took it into working software with Figma MCP, Cursor and Claude Code, so each of us could test what we designed.

    • Product Designer (Apprentice)

      Conversational UX, RAG workflows, power-user research and dashboard design.

    • A team of five

      Worked alongside the Salesforce Trailhead product team.

    • Design to working code

      Figma, Figma MCP, Cursor and Claude Code, wired to OpenAI.

    05Scope and Constraints

    Public documentation only, a brand that can't be faked, and eight real experts.

    We built on OpenAI with curated public documentation and focused on proving the experience. Power users were scarce, so how we tested mattered as much as what we built.

    • Success metric

      Preference over ChatGPT on a real question, session duration, and drop-off after milestones.

    • Guardrail

      Protect Trailhead's warm, human brand. No AI speaking on behalf of a real person.

    • Limits: data and people

      Curated public documentation, not Salesforce's proprietary data or Agentforce, and only 8 real power users.

    06Ideation Steps (The Solution)

    Kill two ideas on purpose, then ship the bet that held.

    Kill the ideas that don't hold, ship the two features that do, then test them as working software instead of a slideshow.

    What we killedTwo ideas we built on purpose, so the real bet had room.

    We explored community status, leverage and skill visibility, then skipped wireframes and vibe-coded the two strongest into working proofs of concept. Trying them ourselves was faster than debating them.

    What we shippedCatch the ChatGPT moment, then give experts a reason to come back.

    Solve catches power users at the moment they would otherwise open ChatGPT; proactive prompts make returning worth it.

    01 · Solve: A plain-language answer, calibrated to you

    • Ask in plain language

      A specific, actionable answer that cites Salesforce documentation.

    • Set the depth

      Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, so you are never over-explained or underserved.

    • Never a dead end

      When Solve can't fully help, it drafts a ready-to-post Trailblazer Community question from your conversation.

    02 · Proactive prompts: The platform does the noticing

    • Renewals

      Flags a certification renewal before it lapses, closing a notification gap users complained about.

    • Skill gaps

      Measures a skill profile against what the market is hiring for and builds a path to close the gap.

    • Follow-ups

      Reads the questions asked in Solve and recommends what to learn next.

    Build & testWorking software and synthetic users before we spent real people's time.

    Each component was designed in Figma against Trailhead's design system, then taken through Figma MCP into Cursor and Claude Code and wired to OpenAI. No handoff, no rebuild from a spec: a working app, so we could test it like a product instead of a slideshow.

    Real power users were scarce, so we turned our research into 12 AI agents and ran them through the flows first. By the time 8 real power users arrived, the obvious rough edges were gone and their time went to what needed a person.

    • What was running

      React front end, Express and SQLite back end, OpenAI for responses, voice input, expertise calibration and community-post drafting.

    • 12 agents, then 8 users

      Synthetic users built from research data caught usability problems before real sessions.

    07Outcomes and Takeaways

    Preferred over ChatGPT, with honest limits on what we proved.

    The honest limit: curated public documentation, not Salesforce's proprietary data or Agentforce, and 8 power users. The next step is a larger test on real support data, where the bet can be proven at scale.

    • 6/8 preferred Solve over ChatGPT

      Same Salesforce question, side by side.

    • 4.5/5 confidence

      In Solve's answers.

    • +35% session duration

      Power users stayed in the content.

    • −22% drop-off after milestones

      Experts who used to leave after a milestone stayed.

    “I had never been comfortable in code, and had never prototyped with it, so teaching myself to ship this as working software is the barrier I'm proudest of clearing.”

    Teammate on this project

    LearningThe hardest part was finding authentic power users.

    • What worked

      Designing for users who'd already arrived gave every decision a clean test.

    • What was hard

      Recruiting authentic power users on a semester timeline.

    • What's next

      Scale testing globally, refine Solve's answer quality, and pitch a formal roadmap.

    “Power users don't disengage because Trailhead runs out of content. They disengage because it stops speaking to who they've become.”

    Advait Ramdasi, teammate
