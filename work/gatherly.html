    02Problem

    One events app has to serve people who go and people who host.

    The brief was an events app in the spirit of Airbnb, designed for an agency in three months. Someone had to be able to find an event and get a ticket, and someone else to host their own.

    03Hypothesis

    Four journeys and one token set are enough for the whole product.

    Cover the product with four key flows, and drive light and dark from a single token set, so a three-month MVP stays consistent.
