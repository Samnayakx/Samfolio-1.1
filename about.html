    About ( Sambit )

    Product designer - engineer

    Hi there, I’m Sambit.

    I’m a product designer building AI-native products from 0 to 1. I’m the founding product designer at CotonAI, an enterprise agentic AI fashion platform, and recently designed Trailhead Solve at Salesforce.
