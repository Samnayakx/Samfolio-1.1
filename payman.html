FAQ

Questions banks and credit unions ask

What is Payman AI?

Payman AI deploys AI agents for banks and credit unions that carry out real banking transactions, such as payments, transfers and account analysis, through voice or text on the institution's existing systems, under policies, approvals and audit trails the institution controls.

Who is it for?

Banks and credit unions, their customers and members, and their staff.

Is it safe for banks and credit unions?

Yes, when built with the right controls. The agent acts only when an authenticated customer or member asks. An immutable policy engine checks every action before it reaches the core, every step is recorded in a complete audit trail, and the institution holds the kill switch.

Is Payman SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. Payman is SOC 2 Type II. Details are at trust.paymanai.com.

Does it replace our core banking system?

No. Payman runs on the institution's existing rails and integrates with existing core banking systems and digital banking platforms rather than replacing them.
