The root cause

Two places defined colour, and they disagreed

index.css held HSL variables for the shadcn layer. boardUiTokens.ts held hex for the board chrome and was imported by 106 files. Where they disagreed, nothing said which was right.

which component for which job, and the three rules

Decision 1 · Palette

Generated, with the brand pinned

One neutral ramp, gray/0 to 1000, and five hues in OKLCH with a fixed lightness per stop. blue/500 #0C8CE9, red/500 #FF383C and purple/600 #8A38F5 are pinned, so the brand never drifts.

Decision 2 · One blue, two jobs

Brand for focus, a darker blue for fills

White on #0C8CE9 is 3.53:1. On #0076C7 it is 4.76:1. Filled actions use blue/600. Focus and selection keep the brand blue.

Decision 3 · Muted text

Darker, so it passes on every row

gray/650 #636360 in light, gray/450 #9F9E9C in dark. Picked so text stays above 4.5:1 even on selected and pressed rows: 4.96:1 on #EAE9E6.

Decision 4 · Shape and size

Three radii, two heights, a 12px floor

Radii are 6, 12 and full. Controls are 32px, with 28px only in dense inspector rows. Anything a person has to read is at least 12px.

Decision 5 · Deprecate

Old names alias to new ones

rounded-md, text-muted and other older names stay as aliases of the new ones. A name is removed only after its last use is gone.

Decision 6 · Who wins

The board's values beat shadcn's

Where index.css and boardUiTokens.ts disagreed, the values on the live board won. One accent colour, no second one.
